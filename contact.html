Everything You Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

We host competitive brackets across all levels: Beginner (Open League), Intermediate (Challenger Division), and Elite (Grand Slam Pro Tier). In addition, we run weekly handicap tiebreak shootouts where players of all ratings can compete on balanced terms.

Solo players are always welcome! You can immediately jump into ranked 1v1 singles matchmaking or enter the random doubles pool where you will be paired with a compatible partner according to your divisional ELO rating.

Player rankings update dynamically based on set win percentage, ace ratios, and head-to-head match outcomes against ranked opponents. The top 16 seeds in every regional tournament receive priority bye slots in major bracket events.

All official ITF regulation rackets and approved tournament balls are standard. Players participating in digital esports leagues are calibrated using certified low-latency input controllers and standardized string physics parameters.

Our Center Court Arena provides dedicated player locker rooms, warm-up practice courts, high-definition broadcast spectator seating, and complimentary on-site parking for all tournament participants.
